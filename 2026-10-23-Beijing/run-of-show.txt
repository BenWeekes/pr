AVATAR-PROTOCOL SLIDE

ConvoAI speech → avatar / video provider → audio + video in an Agora channel.
Agora opens a provider session through REST. The provider returns a WebSocket
address and session token, receives the agent's speech and controls over that
connection, generates video, and publishes audio plus video into the channel.

The vendor implements the session endpoints and WebSocket receiver; the
repository supplies a Go publisher to use or adapt. Gesture actions are
specified as in development, with action handling implemented by the provider.
The slide leads directly into the avatar comparison and use-case videos.

Source: https://github.com/AgoraIO-Solutions/convoai_to_video
Protocol controls:
